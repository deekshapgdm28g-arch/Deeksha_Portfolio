Cash Flow Analysis of Three Corporations

Indirect and direct methodWorking capitalCapital allocation
Read the STAR
Situation
Three firms with very different profiles: a loss-making restructurer, a fast-growing small company, a large firm with falling profit.
Task
Judge financial strength from the cash flow statement alone.
Action
Split operating, investing and financing flows. Reconciled net income to operating cash. Tested whether operations covered capex and dividends. Traced working capital swings and how shortfalls were funded.
